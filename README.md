Intense Indigo started as a binary/hex edit hack by SinisterHoodedFigure (SHF) in 2012.

Years later, the Pret disassembly of Pokemon Red and Blue was completed.

Since then, new Kaizo hacks have been made and becoming more popular. Intense Indigo, due to being older wasn't able to be updated much as binary editing has gone away.

I reached out to SHF about updating Intense Indigo and some of his other games in 2024/2025. Those games have been updated and you can find the patches here: https://github.com/CreamElDudJafar/SHF-Kaizo-Patches

However, with the creation of Yellow Kaizo by SHF and I (2026), I decided the world needs a Intense Indigo disassembly so here it is!

Their is 6 Branches here for you to choose from:
- Intense Indigo 2013- 2013 Version
- Intense Indigo Lite 2013- 2013 Version
- 2 Full GBC Color Lite or Regular QOL- Intense Indigo in full GBC Color. Game can be built with Gen 1 or Gen 2 graphics (Gen 2 has EXP Bar, Caught Indicator Icon)
- 2 Regular QOL- QOL Versions are like Blue Kaizo QOL. Just some minor features like Use HMs from overworld, repels ask to be reused, new items, bag space indicator for space just to enhance the player and or for nuzlockes.
